## How they stand

- Quality score: not comparable (the scores come from different formula versions).
- Engagement rate: example-creator-a is higher than example-creator-c ("similar" means within 15% of the higher rate).

## Read before deciding

- The two accounts are in different follower tiers; rates and scores move with size.
- The engagement rates were measured 10 days apart.
- example-creator-c: the engagement rate is older than 7 days.
- The quality score describes stored public signals. It is not a guarantee of sales, reach or campaign results.
- Nothing here says two accounts share the same people; audience overlap is not measured.
